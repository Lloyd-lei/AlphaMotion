EIGEN AXIS TRANSFER (0811) — NEGATIVE RESULT
Does w_1 fitted on one endpoint pair steer another? rho(alpha, realised psi_1):
scene            self   cross(mean)   random(mean of 3)
walk->kick       0.87      0.21         -0.32
run->sit         0.92     -0.23          0.67
dance->walk      0.96      0.24         -0.28
punch->jump      0.92      0.40          0.19
Self-steering is strong and reproducible (0.87-0.96). Cross-scene steering is
weak (-0.23..0.40) and, critically, NOT separable from the random-direction
control, which itself ranges -0.32..0.67 — a random push in 192 lattice coords
sometimes scores 0.67 by chance. So: the eigen axes are LOCAL to their bridge,
and any claim of a general unsupervised control basis is unsupported.
(The random arm is why this is stated as refuted rather than "weak transfer":
without it, cross=0.40 would have looked like partial success.)
