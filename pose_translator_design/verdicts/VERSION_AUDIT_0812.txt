unmasked path (bridge_b.tokens_of, B3's training contract). Measured overlap
between the two streams: 0.15%. Anything that mixes them is invalid; anything
that stays on ONE of them is internally valid.

CLASS 2 — B-INTERNAL, everything on the unmasked path. VALID as comparisons.

CLASS 3 — GENERATION PATH, B tokens (unmasked) fed to A3's decoder (trained on
masked z). CROSS-CONTRACT BY CONSTRUCTION. Numbers are real measurements of the
system as built and shipped, but they are NOT measurements of the intended
architecture, and a contract-correct build has never been compared.

  homecoming_probe + homecoming_viz, rainbow_bridge, rainbow_eigen,

       it goes in a paper.
