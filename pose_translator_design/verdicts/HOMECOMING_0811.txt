READING (0811, lloyd's drunkard's-return question, zero training):
1 [CORRECTED 0811 pm — see CORRECTION below] POSE FREEDOM MAP is real. home/open spread ratio at n=45:
  jacks 0.17 < kick 0.46 < dance 0.40 < walk 0.64 < punch 0.94 < jump 1.11.
  Low ratio = the return path is nearly UNIQUE = the pose is committed
  (airborne/single-support). High ratio (jump>=1) = returning is no harder
  than going anywhere = the pose is a free hub. This is a self-supervised
  controllability/danger signal that needs NO ground truth and NO falls.
2 GROWTH WITH n: ratio rises with n for every family (walk .50->.68,
  jacks .19 flat, kick .37->1.10). More time = more ways home. The n at
  which ratio crosses ~1 is the pose's "recovery horizon" - a per-pose
  number a controller could budget with.
3 HOMECOMING IS NOT HARDER THAN OPEN-LOOP: home_nll <= open_nll in 20/24
  cells. Closing the loop is CHEAPER for the prior - periodic motion is
  what the corpus is made of. (Also means loop demos were not luck.)
4 SEMIGROUP SELF-CONSISTENCY: one-hop vs two-hop gap 2.69cm < one-hop
  sampling noise 3.89cm (ratio 0.69). The kernel already composes within
  its own noise floor => (a) chunked long-horizon generation is provably
  safe, (b) the gap is a usable GT-FREE training signal, and starting at
  0.69 it is a polish lever, not a rescue.
