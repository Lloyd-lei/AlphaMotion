B6 (goal_drop 0.10 -> 0.50, warm-start B3, 40k steps) — PHASE-SPACE VERDICT

BRIDGING (the thing we were afraid of losing): IMPROVED.
  val bridge CE 3.118 -> 2.944, ppl 22.6 -> 19.0, token acc 0.267 -> 0.290.
  Raising goal dropout 5x did not cost bridging quality. (Part of this is 40k
  extra steps, so it is not a clean ablation of the dropout rate alone.)

AUTONOMOUS FLOW (the thing it was trained for): STILL FAILS, differently.
  return-map slope   corpus -0.70 | B3 -0.09 | B6 +0.90
  B3 collapsed to a fixed point (slope ~0, portrait shrunk to a dot).
  B6 no longer collapses to a point — its portrait covers 2 cm of travel and
  wanders — but the return map slope is +0.90, i.e. NEUTRAL/DRIFTING: each
  cycle lands where the previous one left it, with no restoring force. The
  corpus's -0.70 means real gait actively pulls back to the cycle; B6 does not
  pull back at all. Amplitude is also still an order of magnitude short
  (2 cm of ankle travel vs the corpus's 20 cm; velocity +-0.6 vs +-3).
  Lyapunov: walk +0.002 (neutral), jacks +0.091, sit +0.106 — consistent with
  a marginally stable / slowly diverging flow rather than an attractor.

READING: more goal dropout moved the failure from FROZEN to DRIFTING. That is
progress in the sense that the flow now moves, and it is not progress in the
sense that the gait attractor still does not exist. The missing ingredient is
not exposure to goal-free training; it is that nothing in the objective rewards
returning to a cycle. Masked-token CE is a per-frame likelihood; it has no term
that penalises slow drift of the whole trajectory.
NEXT CANDIDATES, in order of my confidence:
 (a) train ON rollouts, not only on real windows: sample a chunk, re-encode its
     own output as the next chunk's start, and require the chain to stay on the
     data manifold (this is the exposure-bias fix, and it directly targets
     drift);
 (b) lloyd's E17 RL arm, with the return-map slope as the reward — we now have
     a scalar target (-0.7) instead of a vibe;
 (c) longer chunks (n=120) so one chunk contains a full gait cycle and the
     prior can learn the cycle within a single conditioning window.
