  codes -> HUMAN wrist:  p95 ~12cm   (info is in the codes)

  => The arm tail on the G1 ruler is a DATA WALL: human->G1 paired GT (GMR

  motion. The decoder already sits at the ceiling the pairs permit. Neither
  decoder post-train NOR an encoder retrain can buy arm accuracy on this
  yardstick. (Corroborates: GMR arm params were the historic pain point;
  eval-traps memory: suspect measurement first on anomalous numbers.)

Consequences:
- C1 remains Greenwich. PT checkpoint kept as evidence (identical codes).
- The "spatial arm round" as planned (encoder retrain) is CANCELLED on this
  evidence - it attacks the wrong wall.
- Real levers: (a) GMR arm retarget surgery + re-pair, then decoder post-train
  regains signal; (b) yardstick revision: arm acceptance on human-side or
  multi-embodiment consensus instead of single noisy retarget.
- Punch/text confusion is a separate issue (human arm info IS in codes at
  12cm p95): suspect A3 32-token compression or alignment corpus size.

VERDICT ON THE 17% "RECOVERABLE" HEADROOM: refuted as a training-recipe target.
Two post-trains (gentle 20k, hot 30k with a position-specific wrist term) both
moved the wrist tail by <1%. The readout probe's 29.1cm ceiling is reachable by
a free-form MLP on the codes but NOT by this decoder under this objective —
i.e. the gap is not "the decoder is under-trained on arms", it is structural:
the decoder must serve all embodiments through one shared conditional path,
and the probe does not.
Consequence: stop buying arm accuracy with decoder post-training. The remaining
levers are upstream (GMR arm retarget / re-pairing) or a yardstick change
(human-side or multi-embodiment-consensus arm acceptance), exactly as the
first-round verdict said. Greenwich stays C1; both PT checkpoints kept as
evidence that the frozen-lattice surgery is sound and cascade-free.
