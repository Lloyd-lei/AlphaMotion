VERDICT
  The encoder swap is NOT worth anything. Headline zero-shot moves 0.25 deg on a +-0.9 deg seed
  band; the axis-triad errors reshuffle without a direction; and the only decisive measurement
  (limit-violation behavior on unseen bodies) goes to SigLIP. Qwen3 also costs more (1024-d
  conditioning vs 768-d, a causal-LM forward per vocabulary refresh, and a second HF cache).
  Keep SigLIP. The E19-vs-E27_sig gap (2.2 deg, same encoder) says the lever that actually
  moves zero-shot is the training recipe, not the name-embedding source; the hip-axis question
  should be attacked with geometry/DOF conditioning (the use_dof channel), not with a bigger
  text model.
