NOVELTY FRONTIER (0811) — does the bridge reach outside the training set?
frozen Equator A3+B3 + Greenwich C1. 3 endpoint pairs x 10 samples x 6 taus,
60-frame windows, compared against a 3000-window index built from RAW GT human
motion (no model in the index).

reference                                   novelty_cm   jerk
corpus leave-one-out NN  median                  5.62    0.205 (median)
corpus leave-one-out NN  p90                    14.23
CONTROL real motion round-tripped through
  the SAME pipeline (A3 encode->decode->C1)     11.76    0.291
generated, tau=3.0                              20.90    2.577
generated, tau=1.3                              21.44    1.233
generated, tau=0.35                             20.03    0.935

WHAT THE CONTROL CHANGES: without it the raw novelty ~20cm looks like
extrapolation. The control shows ~11.8cm of it is codec+decoder distortion —
real motion pushed through the pipeline already lands that far from itself.
The honest excess is ~8cm, still above the corpus's own p90 spacing (14.2).

TWO AXES, SEPARATELY CAUSED — the useful finding:
  novelty is FLAT in temperature (20.0-21.4 across a 9x tau range) => it is
    induced by the TASK (endpoints drawn from different action families force a
    path no single corpus window contains), not by sampling randomness.
  jerk falls 2.58 -> 0.94 as tau cools => roughness IS the temperature.
So cooling buys smoothness without giving up novelty. But even the coolest
setting is 3.2x rougher than round-tripped real motion (0.935 vs 0.291).

VERDICT on "we found a way to reach outside the training distribution":
PARTIALLY SUPPORTED, NOT PROVEN. The paths are measurably not copies of any
corpus window, and that distance is not a sampling artefact. But they are not
corpus-quality motion either, so "outside the distribution" cannot yet be
distinguished from "inside the distribution plus quality deficit". The claim
becomes safe only if generated jerk reaches the control band while novelty
stays >=20.
