TWO FAILURE MODES, NOT ONE. Every temporal arm to date has been judged against a single
sentence ("the tokens are decorative"), and that sentence hides two independent mechanisms
with two independent fixes. Reading a flat ladder as a verdict on masking, or a flat energy
number as a verdict on the register chain, will get the wrong answer.

  MODE A — MEAN REGRESSION.
    Mechanism : the decoder's conditioning NEVER VARIED. V5 always handed it the same two
                frames (current + target), so the cheapest solution is the per-frame
                conditional mean of all trajectories with those endpoints.
    Symptom   : motion energy below 1.0, worst in the families whose interior is the whole
                point — jumping jacks, run, kick. V5c energy 0.762-0.805; jacks historically
                0.2-0.4, i.e. a jack whose interior has been averaged away.
    Fix       : V6's frame-mask DISTRIBUTION. At `sparse` density there is no endpoint pair to
                interpolate between, so the interior can only come from the registers.
    Rows to read: per-family energy, content share, the mask ladder.

  MODE B — REGISTER REDUNDANCY (the flat OAT ladder).
    Mechanism : the parallel Perceiver readout factorizes the code into per-register
                INDEPENDENT MARGINALS — the readout queries never see each other — so every
                register regresses to the same conditional mean and register 1 alone already
                says what registers 1..8 say.
    Symptom   : the OAT prefix ladder is flat. V5c: 7.66 cm at prefix1, 2, 4 AND 8 (spread
                0.017 cm). V6_mae at 20k: spread 0.010 cm — UNCHANGED, as expected.
    Fix       : --reg_ar (N3a's insight #2). Register k's readout query cross-attends the
                QUANTIZED values of registers < k.
    Rows to read: the prefix1/2/4/8 ladder.
    MEASURED MECHANISM, 2026-08-07 (V6Tokenizer, d=32, identical weights, one perturbation):
      parallel readout : perturb register 0's query -> register 0 moves 2.083,
                         registers 1..7 move EXACTLY 0.0000. They are independent marginals.
      register chain   : perturb the chain prefix -> register 0 moves 0.0 (it precedes the
                         prefix), registers 1..7 move 0.098 / 0.150 / 0.124 / 0.122 / 0.059 /
                         0.093 / 0.046. The chain is real.
    This is why a flat ladder under V6_mae is NOT evidence against insight 3c: V6_mae keeps
    V5's parallel readout verbatim and has no mechanism against Mode B at all.

THE 2x2. Four arms, one control per cell, all at 62.69 bit/s and the same V5c loss recipe:

                        |  no frame masking      |  V6 frame-mask distribution
    --------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------
    parallel readout    |  V5c_0806  (neither)   |  V6_mae_0807      (Mode A only)
    register chain      |  N3a_regAR_0807 (B only)| V6_regar_0807    (BOTH)

  V6_mae_resid_0807 sits outside the 2x2: it is V6_mae + --residual_in, testing the OTHER half
  of insight 3c (rates instead of absolute states) and is read against V6_mae, not against the

OPERATIONAL LESSON FROM TONIGHT — PLAN PARALLELISM ON PEAK, NOT STARTUP, MEMORY.
  The spatial arms (train_dual) grow enormously at their eval checkpoints: N3f went 24.5 -> 46.3
  GB, N3e went 24.5 -> 41.0 GB, both hours after startup. A slot measured at startup is not a
  slot. Concretely tonight: 15.3 GB was freed at ~04:00 and the card read 80.6/97.9; by the time
  the next arm was ready to launch the card read 96.9/97.9, because N3e had absorbed all of it
  and more. Launching on that stale reading would have OOM'd the two V6 arms already in flight.
  V6_regar was therefore gated on a SUSTAINED free-memory measurement (>= 12 GB across 5
  consecutive one-minute polls) rather than on a pid disappearing, and the gate is what delayed
  it. The rule for the next queue: budget every train_dual arm at its EVAL peak (~46 GB), not
  its steady state (~24 GB).

THREE STRUCTURAL DIFFERENCES FROM V5c (nothing else changed)

  1. THE MASK IS A DISTRIBUTION, NOT A CONSTANT. V5's decoder always got exactly two frames
     (current + target). V6 draws the decoder's visible set per sample: endpoints 40% (V5c's
     exact regime, so the comparable eval is trained rather than extrapolated), rand 30%,
     sparse 15%, prefix 15%. A per-frame conditional mean is not a solution to that mixture —
     at `sparse` density there is no endpoint pair to interpolate between, so the interior can
     only come from the registers.

  2. THE ENCODER IS A MASKED AUTOENCODER OVER FRAMES, AND IT NEVER SEES THE CONDITIONING.
     V5's encoder saw all 60 frames AND ingested the two conditioning tokens — the same
     encoder/predictor collusion channel V5d's --ema_target was built to sever. V6's encoder
     sees only what its own mask leaves (mix: none 30%, rand 40%, sparse 15%, prefix 15%) and
     has no access to the boundary states at all.

  3. PIECEWISE-LINEAR BASELINE THROUGH THE VISIBLE FRAMES, generalizing V5's "predict the
     deviation from the straight line between the endpoints". VERIFIED: with the visible set
     {0, k} it reproduces V5Tokenizer.baseline to 2.4e-07 (integer k, which is every training
     step and every harness call); V5's exact continuous-time formula is still used verbatim
     for the zero-bit floor row so the number V5c was judged against is numerically the same.

  Held fixed: 6 key joints, 60-frame window, V5c loss recipe (warp aug linear + Riemann `both`
  + cdrop 0.15/0.05 + --cdrop_time_detach + w_vel 0.5 + w_frame 6.0 + InfoNCE 0.1), 8 path
  registers + 1 time register on the 6x5 FSQ grid, AdamW 3e-4, bs 128, 60k steps.
