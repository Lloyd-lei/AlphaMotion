WHAT HOLDS
 * Direction of the effect is OPPOSITE to my prediction: modes do NOT merge on
   cooling, they RESOLVE. Hot = one undifferentiated cloud (MDS panel tau=4 is
   a structureless blob). Cold = tight, widely separated islands (MDS tau=0.2).
   The prism metaphor is literally right; my "coalescence" guess was wrong.
 * The DEMON signature is clean and assumption-free: unique token paths stay
   48/48 down to tau=0.35, then halve to 24/48 at tau=0.2 — seeds are falling
   into shared basins. No clustering hyperparameter involved.
 * Spread contracts monotonically 9.09 -> 3.09 cm.
 * Rendered modes at tau=0.2 differ visibly in leg phase / lean (see video),
   i.e. the clusters are not pure root drift.

WHAT DOES NOT HOLD (do not quote the mode counts)
 * The eigengap mode count is UNSTABLE at intermediate tau: 5 (1.6) -> 3 (1.0)
   -> 2 (0.6) -> 6 (0.35). The MDS panels show why: at tau=1.0 the cloud is
   still a continuum, so any k is an arbitrary cut. Only at tau<=0.35, where
   the cloud is genuinely discrete, is the count meaningful.
 * A full Gaussian affinity graph can never report >1 mode (one zero
   eigenvalue by construction) — fixed mid-run to union-kNN with Zelnik-Manor
   local scaling. Any earlier "1 mode everywhere" reading was that bug.

USE THE ROBUST PAIR INSTEAD: (unique_token_paths, spread) vs tau. Both are
hyperparameter-free and tell the same story.
